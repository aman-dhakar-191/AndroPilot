# Gallery (Xiaomi)

## Structure

- A grid of photos by date, plus an Albums tab. Opening an item gives a viewer with share,
  edit, and delete actions -- usually as unlabelled icons along the bottom.

## Traps

- The grid is virtualised: items scroll in and out and their ids do not survive scrolling.
  Re-observe after every scroll.
- Delete and share sit next to each other in the viewer's action row. Confirm which is
  which from the screen before tapping.

## Safety

- Never share or delete. Sharing sends private images off the device; deleting is not
  recoverable through this app.
