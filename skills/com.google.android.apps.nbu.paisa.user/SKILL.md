# Google Pay (UPI)

Money. Read this before touching anything here.

## The rule

Every transfer, payment, collect-request approval and autopay mandate is a financial action
and is gated by the device's safety policy. Expect a confirmation to be required, report it,
and stop. Do not look for a way around it -- there is not one, and trying is the wrong
instinct.

## Structure

- A home screen of recent contacts and businesses, a scan control, and a payments area.
  Sending money goes: choose recipient, enter amount, confirm, then a UPI PIN screen.

## Traps

- The UPI PIN screen is a secure keypad. It is for the person holding the phone. Never
  attempt to enter a PIN, and never read one back.
- Recipient lists show similar names and truncated UPI ids. A wrong tap sends real money to
  a real stranger and is not reversible.
- A "collect request" asks the user to pay someone else. Approving one is paying.

## What is safe

- Reading balance, transaction history, and payment status is fine and is usually all that
  is actually being asked for. Do that and report it.
