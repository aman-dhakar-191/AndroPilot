# Camera

## Structure

- A viewfinder with a mode strip (Photo, Video, Portrait, ...) and a shutter control. Most
  controls are unlabelled icons; use role and position, and observe rather than assume.

## Traps

- The shutter is a button whose label is often empty. Identify it by role and its position
  at the bottom centre, and confirm with a fresh observation that a capture happened.
- Switching modes changes what the shutter does -- in Video it starts a recording that then
  has to be stopped.

## Safety

- Taking a photo is not reversible from the agent's side and it captures whatever is in
  front of the lens, including people. Only capture when the goal asked for it explicitly,
  and never "to see what happens".
