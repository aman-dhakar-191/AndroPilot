# Netflix

## Structure

- Rows of artwork with a search entry. A title opens to a detail page with a play control.
  Playback takes over the screen and controls auto-hide.

## Traps

- Profile selection appears on launch and chooses whose watch history is affected.
- Artwork tiles are frequently unlabelled; use the accessibility text or the title on the
  detail page rather than guessing from position.
- Playback changes the device's audio and keeps the screen awake.

## Safety

- Do not start playback unless asked -- it makes noise on somebody's phone.
- Do not change profile, rate titles, or alter My List.
