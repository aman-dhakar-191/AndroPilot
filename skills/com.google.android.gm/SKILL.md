# Gmail

## Structure

- A threaded inbox list, a hamburger for labels/accounts, search at the top, and a compose
  button. A thread opens to messages with reply/forward at the bottom.
- Compose has To, Subject, and a body field, with Send in the top bar.

## Traps

- Multiple accounts are common. The account in use is shown in the top bar or the drawer --
  check it before composing, because the wrong sender address is invisible until after.
- Recipient chips only commit when selected from the suggestion list; text left loose in
  the To field may not become a recipient.
- Threads collapse quoted history; what you can see is not the whole message.

## Safety

- Sending mail is irreversible and is somebody speaking in the user's name. Compose and
  stop for approval; do not tap Send unless sending was the explicit goal.
- Do not archive, delete, or mark read as a side effect of reading. Opening a thread already
  marks it read -- mention that if the goal was only to check.
