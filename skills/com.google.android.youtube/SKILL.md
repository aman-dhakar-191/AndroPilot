# YouTube

## Structure

- Bottom tabs (Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, You) with search in the top bar. A video opens
  to a player with a description below and comments further down.
- The player has a fullscreen control and a set of overlay buttons that auto-hide; tap the
  video once to bring them back before targeting them.

## Traps

- Shorts is a vertical feed that plays immediately and swipes between videos. It is easy to
  end up there by accident from Home.
- Autoplay moves to another video on its own, so the foreground content can change with no
  action from you. Re-observe before trusting an earlier reading.
- Player controls disappear after a few seconds; a selector that resolved a moment ago can
  legitimately vanish.

## Safety

- Do not subscribe, like, comment, or add to playlists -- those are public actions under
  the user's account.
