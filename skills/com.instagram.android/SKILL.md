# Instagram

## Structure

- Bottom tabs (Home, Search, Reels, Shop/Profile) over feeds. Feeds are infinite and
  virtualised, and Reels plays video immediately on entry.

## Traps

- Almost every control is an unlabelled icon and several sit close together -- like,
  comment, share, save. A misplaced tap is a public action.
- Double-tap on a post is a like. Avoid repeated taps on media.
- The feed re-orders and loads as you scroll, so an element from an earlier snapshot may be
  a different post by the time you act on it. Re-observe immediately before acting.

## Safety

- Do not like, comment, follow, share or post. Everything social here is public and
  attributed to the user.
- Reading a story marks it seen and the poster is told. Mention that if it happens.
