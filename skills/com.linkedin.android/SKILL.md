# LinkedIn

## Structure

- Bottom tabs (Home, My Network, Post, Notifications, Jobs) with search at the top. Profiles
  and job posts open as scrollable detail pages.

## Traps

- Connect, Follow, and Message sit together on a profile and are one tap from each other.
- The feed's reaction control opens a picker on long-press and reacts on tap.
- "Easy Apply" starts a job application that submits the user's real profile and can send it
  in a couple of taps.

## Safety

- Do not connect, follow, react, comment, post, or apply to anything. All of it is public
  and professional, under the user's real name.
- Reading profiles is generally safe, but viewing a profile can be visible to its owner.
