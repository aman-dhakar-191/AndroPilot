# Amazon

## Structure

- Search at the top over a home feed; results are a list of products opening to detail
  pages with Add to Cart and Buy Now.
- Cart and account live behind the bottom bar or the hamburger.

## Traps

- "Buy Now" skips the cart and can complete an order with a saved address and payment
  method in one or two taps. It sits immediately below Add to Cart.
- Sponsored results look like organic ones. If the goal names a product, check the title and
  seller rather than taking the first row.
- Prices, availability and delivery dates change per pincode and account -- report what is
  on screen, not what is expected.
- Subscribe & Save and Prime trial prompts appear inside ordinary flows.

## Safety

- Placing an order spends real money. It is a financial action and the device will ask a
  human. Report the total and stop.
- Do not add to cart unless asked; do not remove items already in it.
